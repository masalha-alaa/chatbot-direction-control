Chrome extension

Direction controls for multilingual AI chats

Change the current paragraph—or selected paragraphs—between LTR and RTL while composing, without changing the rest of the message. Individual chatbot messages can also be switched independently.

Features

Simple controls, per paragraph and per message

Composer shortcuts

Use Left Ctrl + Left Shift for LTR/left alignment and Right Ctrl + Right Shift for RTL/right alignment. The shortcut applies to the current paragraph, or to all selected paragraphs.

Per-message direction

Use the alignment controls below a message to switch it independently between LTR/left-aligned and RTL/right-aligned.

Remembered locally

Your per-message direction choices are stored locally in your browser and restored when you revisit the conversation.

Private by design

The extension runs locally. It does not collect, send, upload, or share your messages, settings, or other data with external servers.

Screenshots

Works across supported chatbots

Privacy

Nothing leaves your browser

The extension only needs local browser storage to remember direction choices. It has no analytics, telemetry, accounts, or external data service.
